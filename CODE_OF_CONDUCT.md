# 🤝 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community and projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy competitive machine learning ecosystem.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other engineers and researchers
* Being respectful of differing technical opinions, architectural approaches, and benchmarks
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on models and code
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
* Upholding high ethical standards in competitive AI, preventing test-set data leakage

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Plagiarism or uncredited replication of peer competition architectures

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The repository owner and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.
